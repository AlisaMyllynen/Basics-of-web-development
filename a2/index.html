Why autumn is the best season

The summer crowds are gone, the air is crisp, and the whole shore is painted in yellow and rust. A week at the cottage in autumn is slow on purpose: no schedule, no hurry, just the lake and the forest outside the window.

The gravel road to the cottage is at its best this time of year. Birch leaves cover the verges, the sky changes every ten minutes, and the lake reflects it all.

What a cottage day looks like

Mornings start with coffee by the window and a look at the weather over the water. After that, anything goes. Most days are a mix of the things below.

  • Walking the forest paths and collecting colourful leaves
  • Lighting the fireplace or sauna stove in the evening
  • Cooking slowly: soups, fresh bread and baked apples
  • Reading under a blanket while the rain taps the roof
  • Watching the sunset over the lake with a hot drink

Packing list for an autumn weekend

The weather can change quickly, so layers are the key. Pack warm and waterproof, and bring something cosy for the evenings.

  1. Wool socks and a warm sweater
  2. Rain jacket and sturdy boots
  3. A flashlight, because the evenings get dark early
  4. Tea, hot chocolate and snacks
  5. A good book or a deck of cards
